# Vendor Supply Contract — Boltcraft Supplies Inc.

**Contract ID:** CNT-BOLT-2024
**Effective Date:** 2024-02-01
**Buyer:** Sterling Industrial Works, 4400 Foundry Road, Canton, Ohio, USA
**Vendor:** Boltcraft Supplies Inc.
**Approved Vendor ID:** VEND-1002

## 1. Parties

This agreement is between Sterling Industrial Works ("Buyer") and
Boltcraft Supplies Inc. ("Vendor"), a registered supplier of industrial
fasteners, for the supply of bolts, nuts, and related hardware used in
Buyer's assembly operations.

## 2. Pricing Schedule

The following unit prices apply to items supplied under this contract
and represent the maximum billable rate per unit:

| Item | Unit Price Ceiling |
|---|---|
| Industrial Bolts (M12) | $2.15 per unit |
| Industrial Bolts (M16) | $3.40 per unit |
| Hex Nuts (M12) | $0.85 per unit |
| Washer Sets (M12) | $0.45 per unit |

Given the high-volume, low-unit-cost nature of these goods, no
automatic cost-of-living adjustment applies. Any price change requires
a signed amendment.

## 3. Quantity Terms

Maximum order quantity per purchase order is 10,000 units per line
item, reflecting bulk-order volumes typical of fastener supply. Orders
below 500 units per line item on a single purchase order may not be
split across multiple invoices submitted within a 7-day window; each
purchase order must be billed on a single invoice unless partial
shipment has occurred (see Section 5).

## 4. Payment Terms

Net 45 from invoice date. Payment shall be made only to the bank
account on file with Buyer's vendor records (Account ending **4410**).
Any change to payment account details requires written notice
countersigned by both parties at least 15 business days before the
change takes effect.

## 5. Delivery & Acceptance

Partial shipments against a single purchase order are permitted and
each partial shipment may be invoiced separately, provided each
invoice references the same purchase order number and is accompanied
by its own signed delivery confirmation. Cumulative invoiced quantity
against any purchase order may not exceed the quantity approved on
that purchase order.

## 6. Penalty Clause

Late delivery beyond 5 business days of the confirmed delivery date is
subject to a 1.5% penalty per week, deductible from invoiced amounts.

## 7. Vendor Standing

Vendor is an approved supplier in good standing as of the effective
date. Continued approval is contingent on compliance with Buyer's
Procurement Policy (see `procurement_policy.md`).
