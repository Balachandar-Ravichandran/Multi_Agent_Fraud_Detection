# Sterling Industrial Works — Procurement & Accounts Payable Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-PROC-2024
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Applies to:** All vendor invoices processed by the Finance team

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs how vendor invoices are reviewed and approved for
payment, and defines the controls used to detect billing anomalies
before funds are released.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

Any single purchase order with a total value exceeding **$10,000**
requires secondary approval from the Finance Director in addition to
the standard procurement lead sign-off. This threshold applies to the
combined value of all line items on the purchase order.

## 3. Anti-Splitting Rule

Vendors and internal requisitioners may not divide what is
functionally a single purchase into multiple smaller purchase orders
or invoices for the purpose of avoiding the secondary approval
threshold in Section 2. Any set of invoices from the same vendor,
submitted within a 7-day window, whose combined value would have
triggered secondary approval if billed as one invoice, must be
escalated for review regardless of how the individual invoices were
structured.

## 4. Duplicate Invoice Rule

An invoice is considered a duplicate if it matches a previously
processed invoice on **vendor + purchase order reference + invoiced
amount**, even if the invoice number itself differs. Finance must
check new invoices against the invoice ledger before approving payment,
not solely against the invoice number.

## 5. Delivery Confirmation Requirement

No invoice may be approved for payment without a corresponding signed
delivery confirmation on file for the purchase order it references.
Vendors requiring additional inspection sign-off (see individual vendor
contracts) must have that inspection record on file as well.

## 6. Vendor Bank Account Changes

Payment must only be made to the bank account currently on file in the
Vendor Master record. Any invoice presenting bank account details that
differ from the Vendor Master record must be rejected and escalated to
Finance leadership for manual verification directly with the vendor's
known point of contact — never via the invoice's own listed contact
details.

## 7. Approved Vendor Requirement

Invoices may only be paid to vendors present in the current Vendor
Master list with an "Approved" status. Invoices from unlisted or
suspended vendors must be rejected pending vendor onboarding or
reinstatement review.

## 8. Price Ceiling Enforcement

Invoiced unit prices must not exceed the ceiling defined in the
relevant vendor contract, inclusive of any applicable cost-of-living
adjustment explicitly permitted by that contract. Purchase orders
raised above a vendor's contracted ceiling do not themselves authorize
payment above that ceiling; the contract remains the governing price
reference regardless of what a purchase order states.
