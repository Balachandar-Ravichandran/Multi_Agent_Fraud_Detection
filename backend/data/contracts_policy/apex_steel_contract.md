# Vendor Supply Contract — Apex Steel Components Ltd.

**Contract ID:** CNT-APEX-2024
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Buyer:** Sterling Industrial Works, 4400 Foundry Road, Canton, Ohio, USA
**Vendor:** Apex Steel Components Ltd.
**Approved Vendor ID:** VEND-1001

## 1. Parties

This agreement is between Sterling Industrial Works ("Buyer") and Apex
Steel Components Ltd. ("Vendor"), a registered supplier of structural
steel components, for the supply of steel brackets and reinforcement
hardware used in Buyer's assembly operations.

## 2. Pricing Schedule

The following unit prices apply to items supplied under this contract
and represent the maximum billable rate per unit:

| Item | Unit Price Ceiling |
|---|---|
| Grade-A Steel Brackets | $38.00 per unit |
| Grade-B Steel Brackets | $29.50 per unit |
| Reinforcement Rods (10mm) | $12.75 per unit |
| Reinforcement Rods (12mm) | $15.90 per unit |

Prices may not be exceeded without a signed contract amendment. An
annual cost-of-living adjustment of up to 3% may be applied on the
contract anniversary date (January 1), subject to 30 days' written
notice to Buyer's procurement office.

## 3. Quantity Terms

Maximum order quantity per purchase order is 500 units per line item,
unless pre-approved in writing by Buyer's procurement lead. Orders
exceeding this quantity require a supplementary purchase order with
separate authorization.

## 4. Payment Terms

Net 30 from invoice date. Payment shall be made only to the bank
account on file with Buyer's vendor records (Account ending **7734**).
Any change to payment account details requires written notice
countersigned by both parties at least 15 business days before the
change takes effect.

## 5. Delivery & Acceptance

All shipments require a signed delivery confirmation from Buyer's
receiving dock before the corresponding invoice becomes payable.
Partial shipments against a single purchase order are permitted but
must each be confirmed separately.

## 6. Penalty Clause

Late delivery beyond 10 business days of the confirmed delivery date
is subject to a 2% penalty per week, deductible from invoiced amounts.

## 7. Vendor Standing

Vendor is an approved supplier in good standing as of the effective
date. Continued approval is contingent on compliance with Buyer's
Procurement Policy (see `procurement_policy.md`).
