# Vendor Supply Contract — Meridian Electronics Corp.

**Contract ID:** CNT-MER-2024
**Effective Date:** 2024-03-01
**Buyer:** Sterling Industrial Works, 4400 Foundry Road, Canton, Ohio, USA
**Vendor:** Meridian Electronics Corp.
**Approved Vendor ID:** VEND-1003

## 1. Parties

This agreement is between Sterling Industrial Works ("Buyer") and
Meridian Electronics Corp. ("Vendor"), a registered supplier of
electronic control components, for the supply of control modules and
sensor assemblies used in Buyer's finished equipment.

## 2. Pricing Schedule

The following unit prices apply to items supplied under this contract
and represent the maximum billable rate per unit:

| Item | Unit Price Ceiling |
|---|---|
| Control Module (Type-C) | $145.00 per unit |
| Sensor Assembly (Standard) | $62.50 per unit |
| Sensor Assembly (High-Precision) | $98.00 per unit |

Prices may not be exceeded without a signed contract amendment. No
automatic cost-of-living adjustment applies to this contract.

## 3. Quantity Terms

Maximum order quantity per purchase order is 200 units per line item,
reflecting the calibration and quality-control capacity of Vendor's
production line.

## 4. Payment Terms

Net 30 from invoice date. Payment shall be made only to the bank
account on file with Buyer's vendor records (Account ending **9012**).
Any change to payment account details requires written notice
countersigned by both parties at least 15 business days before the
change takes effect.

## 5. Delivery & Acceptance

Given the precision-sensitive nature of these components, **no invoice
is payable without a signed delivery inspection confirmation** from
Buyer's quality-control team, in addition to the standard receiving
dock confirmation. An invoice submitted without a matching inspection
record on file must be treated as unconfirmed pending verification.

## 6. Penalty Clause

Late delivery beyond 10 business days of the confirmed delivery date
is subject to a 2.5% penalty per week, deductible from invoiced
amounts.

## 7. Vendor Standing

Vendor is an approved supplier in good standing as of the effective
date. Continued approval is contingent on compliance with Buyer's
Procurement Policy (see `procurement_policy.md`).
